# AlmostMine – Own Less. Access More.

**A community-powered peer-to-peer marketplace concept for borrowing, renting, and sharing underutilised belongings.**

## About the Project

AlmostMine is an entrepreneurial application concept built around a simple question:

**Why should we purchase something that we only need occasionally?**

People frequently buy products such as tripods, suitcases, tools, camping equipment and event supplies for temporary use. After serving their purpose, these belongings often remain unused.

At the same time, someone else might need exactly the same item but may end up purchasing a new one.

AlmostMine aims to bridge this gap by creating a community-based platform where people can discover, borrow, rent and share belongings rather than always purchasing them.

Our philosophy:

> Own less. Access more.

## The Problem

Traditional consumption often encourages individuals to purchase products even when their requirements are temporary.

This creates three key challenges:

- **Underutilised belongings:** Products remain unused for extended periods.
- **Unnecessary expenditure:** People spend money purchasing items they may rarely use.
- **Limited accessibility:** People lack a convenient platform to discover belongings available within their communities.

## Our Proposed Solution

AlmostMine proposes a peer-to-peer community marketplace that connects:

- People who own items they rarely use.
- People who need those items temporarily.
- Communities interested in sharing resources and reducing unnecessary purchases.

The platform is designed to make borrowing and renting more convenient, accessible and trustworthy.

## Proposed Application Features

### 1. Community Marketplace
Discover items across categories such as:
- Creator equipment
- Travel essentials
- Household tools
- Electronics and accessories
- Sports equipment
- Occasion and event supplies

### 2. Item Listing Management
A proposed system for users to list, update and manage belongings they are willing to share.

### 3. Booking & Request Management
A structured workflow for sending, accepting, rejecting, cancelling and completing borrowing requests.

### 4. Personalised User Profiles
Customisable user profiles and account preferences designed to support a personalised experience.

### 5. Community Trust System
A proposed rating and review mechanism to encourage accountability and confidence between users.

### 6. Saved Items & Notifications
Features designed to help users save interesting listings and stay informed about relevant activities.

## What Makes AlmostMine Different?

The concept goes beyond a conventional rental marketplace through several proposed features:

| Feature | Intended purpose |
|---|---|
| Idle Value Score | Help owners understand the potential utility of underused belongings. |
| Borrow vs Buy Calculator | Compare the estimated cost of borrowing with purchasing. |
| Community Wishboard | Allow users to express their requirements and reveal unmet demand. |
| Community Trust Profile | Support confidence when interacting with other users. |
| Circular Impact Scorecard | Highlight reuse activity and estimated environmental benefits. |
| Smart Rental Bundle | Help users discover multiple items for a particular occasion. |
| Fair-Price Explainer | Provide context for suggested rental prices. |
| Smart Item Matching | Match user requirements with available listings. |

## Proposed Analytics Framework

An important part of the AlmostMine concept is the use of business analytics to support marketplace decisions.

The proposed analytics framework includes four categories and eight use cases.

### Descriptive Analytics
1. **Marketplace Performance Dashboard:** Track listings, booking activity, completion rates and category performance.
2. **Idle Asset & Community Savings Report:** Understand item reuse, estimated borrower savings and potential owner earnings.

### Diagnostic Analytics
3. **Booking Drop-off Investigator:** Understand booking rejections, cancellations and delays.
4. **Item Demand & Pricing Gap Analysis:** Identify differences between available supply and user demand.

### Predictive Analytics
5. **Seven-Day Category Demand Forecast:** Estimate future demand across different item categories.
6. **Booking Cancellation Risk Prediction:** Identify booking characteristics associated with possible cancellations.

### Prescriptive Analytics
7. **Smart Listing & Pricing Recommendations:** Suggest potential listing and pricing improvements.
8. **Marketplace Growth Action Planner:** Identify opportunities to improve marketplace supply, demand and engagement.

*These are proposed analytics capabilities. They require suitable application data and implementation before they can produce validated real-world insights.*

## Current Development Status

AlmostMine is currently an early-stage application prototype built to demonstrate the proposed product concept, user interface, navigation and planned marketplace experience.

**Current scope:**
- Application concept and product design.
- Homepage and navigation structure.
- Proposed marketplace workflows.
- Proposed user profile and personalisation features.
- Proposed business analytics framework.
- Firebase integration setup.

**Not yet implemented or validated:**
- Populated marketplace database.
- Real registered users and item listings.
- Actual borrowing and rental transactions.

- Live business analytics based on marketplace activity.

The application is a prototype and does not currently represent an operational marketplace.
